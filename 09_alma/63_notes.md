### Alma 63:1–13 — Custody of the Plates and Leadership Succession

Following the conclusion of the wars and the death of Helaman (Alma 62:52), sacred custody of the Nephite records fell upon Helaman's brother **Shiblon**:

> “And it came to pass in the commencement of the thirty and sixth year of the reign of the judges over the people of Nephi, that Shiblon took possession of those sacred things which had been delivered unto Helaman by Alma.” (Alma 63:1)

Shiblon was a just and humble man who walked uprightly before God. However, he kept the records for only three years before his own death in the thirty-ninth year (63:10).

Before his demise, Shiblon conferred the records upon his nephew **Helaman**, the son of Helaman (63:11). Young Helaman was still relatively young, but he had matured enough to bear the weight of the plates.

#### The Generational Power Vacuum
Within a brief span of roughly five years, the entire premier tier of Nephite leadership died:
- **Captain Moroni**: Died in the 36th year (Alma 63:3), only two years after retiring.
- **Helaman**: Died in the 35th year (Alma 62:52).
- **Shiblon**: Died in the 39th year (Alma 63:10).
- **Pahoran**: Died shortly thereafter in the 40th year (Helaman 1:1).

These deaths, coupled with the staggering casualties sustained across fourteen years of warfare, created an acute leadership void. That vacuum severely weakened the central government and church depth, setting the stage for the political fracturing and secret combinations that emerge immediately in Helaman 1–2.

### Alma 63:5–9 — Hagoth and the Great Northern Migrations

During this period of post-war exhaustion and economic realignment, an exceedingly curious man named **Hagoth** built an exceedingly large ship on the borders of the land Bountiful near the Narrow Neck of Land and launched it forth into the West Sea (63:5):

- **Maritime Voyages**: Hundreds of Nephites and Ammonites boarded with provisions and sailed northward.
- **Lost Ships**: Some returned to replenish provisions and set sail again; other ships sailed into the Pacific and were never heard from again (63:7–8).
- **Landward Colonization**: Many others migrated into the land northward by foot.
- **Corianton**: Alma’s youngest son Corianton, having fully repented of his moral transgressions in Alma 39–42, was among those who journeyed northward in a ship to carry provisions unto the colonists (Alma 63:10).

### The Book of Alma in Retrospect

As you think back over the entire book of Alma, which threads do you find most significant and meaningful? How have those threads laced all 63 chapters together into a unified masterpiece?

#### Unifying Threads Across 63 Chapters
1. **The Democratic Experiment**: The transition from monarchy to the reign of judges instituted by King Mosiah is tested by internal dissidents (Nehor, Amlici, Korihor, Zoramites, Amalickiah, Pachus) and external invasions.
2. **True Conversion vs Dissent**: The contrast between the faithfulness of the Anti-Nephi-Lehies and the bitter apostasy of Nephite defectors who became more hardened than the Lamanites themselves.
3. **Priesthood and Sacred Covenants**: Alma’s foundational teachings on the holy order of the high priesthood (Alma 13), the resurrection and spirit world (Alma 40), and justice and mercy (Alma 42).
4. **Covenant Warfare**: The righteous principles of defense under Captain Moroni, Lehi, and the stripling warriors of Helaman.

#### The Pride Cycle and Chiasmus in Alma 62:48

How and when does the pride cycle circulate through the book of Alma? In Alma 62:48–51, Mormon presents a concise chiastic summary that illustrates how humility can reverse the cycle before destruction strikes:

```text
    A The people did establish the church again (Alma 62:46)
        B The church did multiply and grow (62:47)
            C The people did wax proud because of their riches (62:48)
                D BUT they did not forget the Lord their God (62:49)
            C’ They did humble themselves in the depth of humility (62:49)
        B’ The Lord did prosper them in the land (62:50)
    A’ They were blessed with great peace (62:51)
```

#### Preparing Readers for the Final Destinies of the Book of Mormon

As you think ahead to the conclusion of the Nephite record, Mormon has meticulously laid the groundwork to prepare readers to understand what will ultimately unfold in 3 Nephi, Mormon, and Moroni:
- The catastrophic consequences of pride, inequality, and dissension.
- The absolute necessity of individual repentance, covenant fidelity, and family bonds.
- The assurance that God’s covenant promises will never fail, even across the fall of empires.
