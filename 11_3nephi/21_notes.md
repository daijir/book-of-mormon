### 3 Nephi 21:1–7 — The Coming Forth of the Book of Mormon as the Sign

In 3 Nephi 21, Jesus reaches the pivotal core of His Covenant Sermon delivered on Day 2 at the temple in Bountiful. The Lord declares that He will give a specific, tangible "sign" so that all may know that the work of the Father has already commenced in fulfilling the eternal covenant made unto the house of Israel (3 Nephi 21:1–2, 7).

That sign is the coming forth of the Book of Mormon to the Gentiles, who will then carry it unto the remnant of Lehi's seed and the scattered remnants of Israel:

> “And when these things come to pass that thy seed shall begin to know these things—it shall be a sign unto them, that they may know that the work of the Father hath already commenced unto the fulfilling of the covenant which he hath made unto the people who are of the house of Israel.” (3 Nephi 21:7)

Nephi as record keeper, and Mormon as compiler and abridger, recognized this chapter as the central justification for their labors. Mormon sacrificed his life to preserve these records because Jesus had specifically prophesied that their emergence would be the unmistakable signal that the final gathering of Israel had begun.

### The Chiastic Climax of the Covenant Sermon

In the overall chiastic structure of Jesus' Covenant Sermon across 3 Nephi 20:10–22:17 (identified by Victor L. Ludlow), 3 Nephi 21 constitutes the central turning point and apex:

```text
    A The Father and Son work together (3 Nephi 20:10)
      ...
        I Micah quoted: Remnants will be gathered (3 Nephi 20:16–21)
          J All the prophets have testified of Christ (3 Nephi 20:23–46)
            K A key “sign” given, when these things are “about to take place” (3 Nephi 21:1)
              L Zion will be established; work of the Father commences (3 Nephi 21:7)
                M The Father’s servant shall be “marred” (Isaiah 52:14; 3 Nephi 21:10)
              L’ The work of the Father commences among all the dispersed (3 Nephi 21:26)
            K’ The sign given: when the Gentiles shall know the Lord (3 Nephi 21:26–29)
          J’ Search the prophets; Samuel’s missing record restored (3 Nephi 23:1–14)
        I’ Micah and Isaiah quoted: Remnant of Jacob among Gentiles (3 Nephi 21:12–21)
      ...
    A’ The Father and Son work together (3 Nephi 22:14–17; 23:4–5)
```

### 3 Nephi 21:8–11 — The Father's Servant Who Is "Marred"

At the very center of this discourse (3 Nephi 21:10), Jesus quotes Isaiah 52:14 concerning the Lord's servant who shall be "marred" more than any man, yet through whom the Lord will work "a great and a marvelous work" (21:9):

> “For in that day, for my sake shall the Father work a work, which shall be a great and a marvelous work among them ... yet he shall be healed, for their wisdom shall not hurt him.” (3 Nephi 21:9–10)

This prophetic portrait anticipates both the Savior’s own suffering and triumph, and the latter-day prophetic instrument through whom the records would be translated and published despite severe opposition.

### 3 Nephi 21:12–21 — The Remnant of Jacob and Micah's Prophecies

Jesus continues quoting from Micah 5:8–15:
- **The Remnant of Jacob**: "My people who are a remnant of Jacob shall be among the Gentiles, yea, in the midst of them as a lion among the beasts of the forest" (21:12; quoting Micah 5:8).
- **Consecration and Judgment**: If the Gentiles do not repent, the Lord's hand will be lifted up upon their adversaries. All priestcrafts, witchcrafts, images, and idolatries will be cut off, establishing true righteousness.

---

### 3 Nephi 21:22–29 — Assisting in the Building of the New Jerusalem

Jesus outlines the glorious destiny of those who repent and heed the Book of Mormon:
1. **Adoption into the Covenant**: If the Gentiles repent, they will be numbered among the remnant of Jacob and will not be cast out (21:22).
2. **Building the Holy City**: They will assist the remnant of Jacob to build a city to be called the **New Jerusalem** (21:23; see also 3 Nephi 20:22).
3. **The Gathering of All Remnants**: The work of the Father will commence among all the dispersed of His people throughout the earth, bringing them home to the land of their inheritance (21:26–28).
